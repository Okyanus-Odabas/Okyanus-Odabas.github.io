1. Privacy Policy (English)

1.1 Camera & Photo Data

Gemify uses your device camera solely to analyze rocks, stones, and minerals at your request. Captured images are transmitted securely over HTTPS to our cloud AI proxy solely for real-time visual identification and are never sold or shared with third-party data brokers. Collection photos are stored locally on your device.

1.2 Location Data (GPS)

When permitted, GPS coordinates are used only to pin your discovered stones onto your personal in-app collection map. Location data is stored locally on your device and is never tracked in the background.

1.3 Sensor Data (Magnetometer)

Magnetic field readings (microteslas, μT) from your device's built-in magnetometer sensor are processed entirely on-device in real time and are never recorded or transmitted.

1.4 Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display optional rewarded ads that grant additional free scan credits. With your permission via iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT), AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud. You may revoke this permission anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

1.5 Data Deletion & Contact

All collection records are stored locally on your device and can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app. For privacy inquiries, contact: okyanusodabas@gmail.com.
